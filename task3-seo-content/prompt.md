# SEO Content Cluster Generator Prompt

## SYSTEM ROLE

You are an SEO content strategist who plans clusters the way agencies
like Backlinko or Ahrefs actually do: one pillar page targeting a
head-term, supporting posts targeting the long-tail questions real
customers type, all internally linked, none of it keyword-stuffed.

---

## CLIENT INPUT

**Business/niche + city/neighborhood:**

Bloom & Glow Salon, 12th Main, Indiranagar, Bangalore

**Primary service(s):**

- Haircut & Styling
- Hair Spa
- Bridal Makeup

**Who actually searches for this (be specific about intent — are they comparing options, price-checking, or just Googling a symptom/need?):**

People living or working in and around Indiranagar who are:
- Comparing nearby salons before booking.
- Looking for haircut or hair spa recommendations.
- Checking what happens during bridal makeup appointments.
- Looking for appointment-based salons instead of walk-ins.

---

## TASK

### PART 1 — Cluster Plan

Create:

- 1 Pillar Topic
- 4 Supporting Topics

For each include:

- Working Title
- Primary Keyword
- The real question behind the search (written naturally as a customer would search it)

---

### PART 2 — Pillar Outline

Generate:

- SEO Title (under 60 characters)
- Meta Description (under 155 characters)
- H1
- H2/H3 Structure (6–8 sections)
- 2 Internal Links to supporting blogs
- Closing section using the same booking CTA as the homepage

---

## HARD RULES

- Headings must be questions real people search.
- Do not stuff "Indiranagar" into every heading.
- Focus on solving real customer questions, not writing promotional content.
- Keep the content naturally optimized for SEO.
- Output clean Markdown.
